Does crowd attention predict later citations?

Question. Do community upvotes on Hugging Face Daily Papers predict the later citation count of an Arxiv machine learning paper, and which papers does the crowd overrate or underrate?

Answer. Yes as a predictor, not as a cause. Adding upvotes to a leakage checked controls only model raises the forward in time AUC by {{num:lg_dauc}} {{num:lg_dauc_ci}}. Each doubling of upvotes goes with about {{num:nb2_irr}} times the citations (negative binomial, release month, day of week and subfield fixed effects, leakage free prestige), with the Poisson estimate {{num:pois_irr}} and the OLS scale ratio {{num:ols_ratio}} as the range across estimators. The crowd over rewards newer teams and under rewards established ones. Association and prediction only, upvotes are cumulative at collection rather than a day one signal, and the attempted crowding instrument is uninformative.

How to read this dashboard

The data. 11344 Arxiv machine learning papers featured on Hugging Face Daily Papers, each with its cumulative upvote count, its Semantic Scholar citation count from one collection snapshot, its subfield (a uniform 13 level keyword taxonomy), release month, and leakage free author prestige (prior papers and years active of the first or last author before the paper). Papers younger than 5 months or older than 40 months, or without a citation record, are excluded.

The headline is predictive, not causal. "{{num:lg_dauc}} AUC" means: when a logistic regression that already knows a paper's subfield, release month, day of week, prestige and text metadata is also told its upvotes, it ranks 2025 papers by top decile citation status better, AUC {{num:lg_ctrl_auc}} to {{num:lg_att_auc}}, in a strict forward in time test (trained on papers up to 2024, tested on 2025 papers, interval from a paired bootstrap clustered by release month). Histogram gradient boosting gives {{num:hg_dauc}} {{num:hg_dauc_ci}}. Log upvotes alone reach AUC {{num:lg_up_auc}}. This says attention carries information. It does not say attention causes citations.

The association is measured with counts. The incidence rate ratio per doubling of upvotes is {{num:nb2_irr}} {{num:nb2_irr_ci}} under a negative binomial (NB2) with release month, day of week and subfield fixed effects and leakage free prestige. Poisson quasi likelihood gives {{num:pois_irr}} and the OLS log scale ratio {{num:ols_ratio}}, so the estimator matters and the three are shown together.

The explorer is descriptive. Each paper is placed by two percentiles. Horizontal: its attention percentile, the rank of its log upvotes after removing what log age, subfield and release month explain. Vertical: its impact percentile, the same for log citations. A paper in the top third of impact but the bottom third of attention is labelled underrated (violet, n {{num:n_under}}). The mirror image is overrated (green, n {{num:n_over}}). The cut lines are tertiles, the labels are a rule, not a model. Each of the other {{num:n_neutral}} papers is a small grey dot, the black line is the mean impact percentile in each attention bin. Hover for details, click to open the paper on Arxiv, or type part of a title or an Arxiv id to find a paper.

What it cannot tell you. Only featured papers are observed, so there is no pool of submitted but not featured papers. Upvotes are a cumulative count. Citations come from one snapshot, so 2025 papers are still accruing them and the leaderboards default to papers at least 12 months old with at least one citation. The h-index is measured today and is downstream of the citations it is compared with. The crowding instrument is an attempted design whose weak instrument robust interval is too wide to be informative.

Attempted crowding instrument: uninformative

Elasticity of log citations on log upvotes. OLS with release month, day of week and subfield fixed effects and leakage free prestige: {{num:iv_ols}} (n {{num:iv_n}}). The 2SLS with the same fixed effects, instrumenting upvotes with same day crowding from other subfields: {{num:iv_b}} (SE {{num:iv_se}}), Anderson Rubin 95% CI {{num:iv_ar}}, first stage t {{num:iv_t}} and KP F {{num:iv_f}}. The interval is 0.90 log points wide, includes the OLS point and barely excludes zero, so it says nothing about the size of the effect. The day fixed effects version ({{num:iv_day_b}}, KP F {{num:iv_day_f}}, hollow marker) has a strong first stage only because of the within day adding up identity, so it is not exogenous variation. This is an attempted design, not causal evidence.

Limits

  • Predictive, not causal. The headline is out of sample predictive lift, the association ladder is adjusted association with E-values as sensitivity summaries. Nothing here identifies a causal effect of attention on citations.
  • Cumulative upvotes. Upvotes are counts at collection, not day one attention. Late accrual looks small (upvotes and age barely correlate within 2024 and 2025, Spearman {{num:sp_up_age}} by year for 2023, 2024, 2025), but a paper that later became famous may have gathered upvotes after the fact.
  • Single citation snapshot and right censoring. 2025 papers had 5 to 17 months to accrue citations, 2023 papers up to 40. Labels and rankings are age adjusted, but zero counts for young papers are noisy and some are Semantic Scholar matching failures.
  • h-index measured today. Any h-index shown is downstream of the citations it is compared with. Leakage free prestige (prior papers and years active before the paper) is used in every model, and h-index rows are flagged.
  • Keyword subfields. The 13 subfields come from keyword rules on titles, abstracts and Hugging Face keywords, and the never trending papers are labelled from titles and Arxiv categories only, so the subfield match in the control comparison is coarse.
  • Instrument not identified. The crowding instrument's weak instrument robust interval is uninformative and its strong looking day fixed effects variant is mechanical.
  • Only featured papers. There is no pool of submitted but not featured papers, so selection into being featured is not modelled and results do not generalise beyond Hugging Face Daily Papers.
